# Scientific Photometry

Respond in English. Preserve valid pixel values and units. Model sky background robustly while excluding sources, saturation and invalid samples. Distinguish background structure from instrumental artifacts; do not remove faint emission merely because it is smooth or correlated. For this clipping tool, use pixel-derived candidates only and treat structural/scaling/null cards as decoding metadata, not photometric evidence. Compare aperture fluxes, sky estimates and uncertainties before/after processing on unmasked sources; include edge stars, extended objects, PSF wings, gradients and faint injected sources. Crop mode starts at the exact largest clean axis-aligned rectangle; an explicit maximum residual flagged-pixel fraction can permit bounded larger rectangles. Original undefined samples remain hard exclusions. Count sacrificed valid pixels and retained detector flags separately, verify `NAXIS1/2`, and report the bounded search domain. Apply configured utility and crop-sacrifice limits; if no candidate passes, keep the source via symlink without modifying it. The search is not globally optimal among all residual-tolerant rectangles, and a circle-like field can lose samples under the rectangular product contract. Track masked fractions for source and background apertures and flag incomplete measurements. Do not replace rejected values with apparently valid zeros. Quantify selection bias and document uncertainty and evidence in `input/doc/`.

Call unlabeled pixels suspected artifacts or unclassified sacrificed pixels; they are not ground-truth unusable/valid areas. Separate original undefined samples from new loss. Measure lost detections as well as the fluxes of survivors, and compare backgrounds under independently specified aperture/annulus policies. Source masks protect estimator fits and decision constraints; they do not authorize deleting sources or interpolating their flux. Freeze source-loss and completeness budgets before tuning a mask-versus-crop policy. Edge-connected bright stars, galaxies and smooth, still-illuminated vignetting are known confounders of contrast-plus-correlation detectors. Build a pixel-derived source mask (seeded at high S/N, dilated by a pixel-estimated FWHM) that excludes sources from background fits and forbids their removal unless they lie inside an independently plateau-like artifact region. Report every protected-source intersection. Validate source seeds against noise in a local ring (peak > k sigma of ring second-difference noise); otherwise noise clumps in amplified-noise regions become protected "sources" and punch holes in artifact masks. Estimate noise for dispersion tests from second differences (gradient-immune). Pair every significance threshold with a physical effect size; for illumination this is the D4 boundary, where the noise ratio is about eta for raw data and 1/eta after flat division.
