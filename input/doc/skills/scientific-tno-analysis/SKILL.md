# Scientific Tno Analysis

Respond in English. Preserve faint moving sources and non-sidereal trails; do not classify them as defects solely from low signal-to-noise or unusual shape. Preserve exposure timing and declared time scales, cadence, observatory location and astrometric frame. When ephemerides are used, distinguish topocentric, geocentric and barycentric quantities and propagate positional uncertainty. For occultations, protect target/comparison-star apertures and timing metadata. Test injected moving targets at borders across representative fluxes and trails, measure recovery and mask-induced selection bias. This role does not imply adding orbit fitting or ephemeris services to a clipping tool. Record limitations and diagnostic products.
